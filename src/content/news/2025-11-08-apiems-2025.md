本實驗室指導教授張秉宸老師於2025年11月7日至11日率博士班郭玟慧 、碩士班研究生蔡昀宸、徐侑汝，參加 2025 The 25th Asia-Pacific Industrial Engineering and Management Systems Conference (APIEMS 2025) ，並發表論文

- Simulation-based reliability and conditional evaluation of subprojects under time and budget constraints
- System reliability evaluation of manufacturing networks under material variation
- MP-based simulation approach for estimating the reliability of supply chain networks under node-level budget constraints
